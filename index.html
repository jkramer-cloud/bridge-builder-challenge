Construction briefing

Engineering tips

Start with the road joints. Make triangles by placing a joint below the road and connecting it to two neighboring road joints. Steel is stronger, but more expensive. Cables only provide limited bracing in this simplified simulation.

Click a beam in Erase mode to remove it. You can revise failed bridges and test again without losing progress.
