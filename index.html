How to build a bridge

  1. Pick Triangle helper. Click the numbered road span you want to support. It installs bracing below the road, or above once the overhead truss unlocks at mission 9.
  2. Choose materials. Wood is inexpensive; steel costs more but carries heavier loads. Cable and chain resist pulling but go slack when pushed. Concrete withstands pushing but is weak in pulling. Aluminum is light, while wood is cheap and steel is strong in both directions.
  3. Draw beam (optional). Click a joint, then another joint to connect them. Click an empty spot to add a joint. Use Change beam material to replace just one leg with chain, cable, concrete, etc. Use Erase to remove beams.
  4. Test crossing. Watch the moving vehicle and bridge bowing. A force diagram and a question about your own design appear after every test; answer once to continue. You can retry freely.
  5. Inspect. Click Select beam to see a beam’s material and estimated force role; click again or press Escape to deselect. Clicking the selected material again also turns it off. The Weakest Span button highlights a location rather than automatically constructing anything.
  6. Improve. Change your design and test again. Editing always cancels a previous pass. The next mission only unlocks after the vehicle makes it fully across.

48 missions use vehicles from bicycles and small cars to school buses, semis, tanks, and trains. Each region includes new challenges. The numbers are scaled for gameplay, not real bridge design.
