How to build a bridge

  1. Pick Triangle helper. Click the numbered road span you want to support. It installs bracing below the road, or above once the overhead truss unlocks at mission 9.
  2. Choose materials. Wood is inexpensive; steel costs more but carries heavier loads. Cable helps tension bracing. Concrete carries compression well but is heavy (unlocked at level 5).
  3. Draw beam (optional). Click a joint, then another joint to connect them. Click an empty spot to add a joint. Use Erase to remove unwanted beams.
  4. Test crossing. Watch the moving vehicle, colored stress bars, bridge bowing, and possible failure.
  5. Improve. Change your design and test again. Editing always cancels a previous pass. The next mission only unlocks after the vehicle makes it fully across.

48 missions use vehicles from bicycles and small cars to school buses, semis, tanks, and trains. Each region includes new challenges. The numbers are scaled for gameplay, not real bridge design.
